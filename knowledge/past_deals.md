# Past Deal Notes

## Closed-won: mid-market SaaS, VP-level champion

A VP of Sales at a 250-person SaaS company reached out after a
competitor's contract renewal fell through. Budget was pre-approved
($40k/yr) and the timeline was driven by the competitor contract's
expiration date. Closed in three weeks — fast cycles are common when the
lead is already actively replacing an incumbent rather than evaluating
from scratch.

## Closed-won: technology company, bottom-up then exec sponsor

A Director of Engineering at a 500-person technology company first
inquired with a vague "just exploring" note. Follow-up revealed a real
project (migrating an internal tool) with a VP sponsor who joined the
second call. Lesson: thin initial notes don't always mean low intent —
firmographic fit (title, company size, industry) can outweigh weak notes
at first contact.

## Closed-lost: budget mismatch

A Head of Marketing at a 30-person company expressed strong interest and
a clear use case, but budget authority topped out at $2k/yr — well below
our minimum deal size. Good qualitative fit, bad quantitative fit;
closed-lost after two calls once true budget became clear.

## Closed-lost: wrong geography

A promising-looking enterprise lead (large company, senior title, clear
use case) turned out to be based outside our served geography and
required in-region data residency we don't support. Reinforces why
geography is a hard filter rather than just a scoring signal — no amount
of other fit compensates for it.
